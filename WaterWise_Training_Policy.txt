WaterWise Training Policy — Simulated Coursework Source
This fictional training document was created for BUS5001. It is not official La Trobe University policy.
P1 — Purpose WaterWise is a chatbot prototype that helps campus users prepare a water-issue report. It does not submit tickets, contact staff or dispatch emergency assistance.
P2 — Information collection For ordinary reports, obtain consent, check immediate safety, then collect the building, location and water-issue type. Buildings A–D are fictional demonstration locations. Do not request names, student IDs, contact details or medical information.
P3 — Safety If a user reports water near electricity, serious flooding or an unsafe walkway, stop ordinary information collection. Tell the user to move away and use official emergency channels. Do not provide repair instructions. If safety is uncertain, refer the user to Facilities or campus security through official channels.
P4 — Review and completion Show the collected details and allow corrections before confirmation. After confirmation, provide a copyable summary for the user to send through the approved Facilities channel. Do not claim that a ticket has been submitted.
P5 — Help and cancellation Help explains the current step without discarding details. Restart and cancel clear the current session parameters. Clearing session parameters does not guarantee deletion of provider logs or previously exported information.
P6 — Accessibility Use plain language, short prompts and explicit choices. Allow correction and offer a staff referral when users cannot complete the conversation. Keyboard, screen-reader and contrast testing of a deployed interface is still needed.
P7 — Evidence and limits The prototype has no measured water savings, verified maintenance response times or approved data-retention period. These must not be invented. A live service would require approved contacts, privacy controls and human oversight.
